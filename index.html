How to use the lab

Drag the middle of a side to move it. Drag either end to rotate around the other end. Bring loose ends close together, then release to join them. Connected sides stay attached. Use “Separate sides” to start again.

Keyboard: Tab to a side or endpoint. Arrow keys move a side; left/right arrows rotate an endpoint (up/down also rotate). Hold Shift for smaller steps. Press Enter to join nearby ends. A completed triangle moves as one shape.

Think about it: What happens when the two shorter sides add up to exactly the longest side?
